### Supporting data and limitations

- Check production `SITE_ANALYTICS` binding, dataset and `ANALYTICS_ENABLED` before relying on reports. Collection is not verified by source inspection. Creating credentials or changing account settings is separate work.
- Use Analytics Engine for aggregate contact actions by page and language. The report already weights `_sample_interval`. These are events, not unique visitors or linked journeys.
- `form_success` means Resend accepted a submission request. It is not a unique lead, qualification event or confirmation of inbox delivery. Replays may increase counts; public browser events can be fabricated.
- The submitted `source_path` identifies the form page, not the earlier page or external source that acquired the visitor. The dataset stores no referrer, query string or visitor identity.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics provides available traffic/referral context; keep the existing single beacon. Do not join its totals to enquiry rows as if identities were known.
- Use Search Console for indexing, page/query clicks, impressions and country context. Separate branded searches. Query reporting can be incomplete and cannot identify which searcher became a client. [Search Console reporting](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en).
- Archive weekly aggregate reports privately before the custom dataset's three-month retention removes early data. Reports use UTC; label windows consistently rather than silently mixing with Stockholm dates.
- Review for approximately 15 minutes weekly. Compare monthly counts, but inspect enquiry cohorts separately because projects can close later. Calculate qualification and paid-project progression from the deduplicated register. Never divide submissions by search clicks and call that an organic conversion rate.

Available baseline values are currently unknown. Record missing data as missing, not zero. A minimal weekly review shows enquiries, qualified opportunities, agreed scopes, received payments, acquisition effort and the main obstacle. Set financial targets only after confirming delivery capacity and commercial terms.

## Continue, change or pause

These are practical operating thresholds, not statistical benchmarks or research predictions.

| Experiment | Continue | Change | Pause further investment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow automation | One paid project or two qualified enquiries during the initial review period. | Relevant buyers recognize the problem but misunderstand scope, proof or delivery; revise those points. | After five relevant buyer conversations, no suitable needs emerge and no qualified enquiries arrive; revisit before creating more content. |

If exposure is too small or indexing unverified, mark the outcome inconclusive. Allow one additional month of modest validation rather than deleting pages or expanding publication. Track objections before interpreting lack of payment as lack of demand.

Before substantial content, advertising or platform-specific investment: gather real buyer feedback, inspect actual Search Console queries and check result-page intent for the proposed phrases. Keyword volume/competition research is optional additional validation; Trends alone justifies neither a large content programme nor a platform specialty.
