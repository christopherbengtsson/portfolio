Prioritize workflow automation and integrations for remote clients

Recommendation: first test a clear English offer for business workflow automation, with a Swedish counterpart. Strengthen your existing integration pages next. Keep AI-built application review and repair as a small experiment. Your location should not constrain the audience: this research covers worldwide demand, country comparisons and both website languages.

Google Trends measures relative search interest. It cannot establish how many clients will hire you, their budgets, SEO competition or conversion rates. The priorities below combine the observed searches with your existing experience and availability for smaller, clearly scoped projects.

Buying intent and language

Worldwide, all-category phrase searches for "automation consultant", "API integration services" and "code review services" had 12-month average indices of 35, 6 and 0. Automation consultant also includes industrial automation; the 90-day related results included that phrase. It is supporting context, not a clean measure of software buyers.

Swedish searches for systemintegration, automatisering and kodgranskning were sparse. All-category 12-month averages were 2, 4 and 0, and the main related query for automatisering concerned iPhone automation. Broad systemutvecklare had more interest than webbutvecklare, but rising queries concerned education and jobs. Avoid making generic career or beginner content the acquisition strategy.

The site already has English and Swedish service pages. English can reach international buyers and buyers searching in English within Sweden. Swedish pages should use natural commercial language and business problems, rather than literal translations alone. These requests do not establish which language Swedish clients prefer.

Exploratory "AI automation consultant" showed interest under all categories, but insufficient data in Computers & Electronics; "n8n consultant" and "Lovable developer" were also sparse. Broad Lovable or vibe coding popularity does not prove paid demand for app repair. Some related-query lists contained clearly irrelevant searches even after filtering. Those entries were excluded from recommendations.

The terms in the plan are proposed targeting hypotheses, not measured keyword volumes or validated conversion rates. Do not claim n8n, Zapier or Lovable specialization without demonstrable work. Preserve your broader software developer positioning and make remote delivery and smaller project availability explicit.

Suggested first proof article: “When should you use an automation tool versus a custom API integration?” / “När passar ett automationsverktyg och när behövs en egen API-integration?” Explain cost drivers, exceptions, maintenance and when a developer is needed, then link to the relevant service and project form. This is a content hypothesis intended to help a buying decision.

Each offer should state the problem, a concrete deliverable, scope boundaries, relevant experience, handover and one project enquiry action. Ask visitors which tools are involved, what happens today and what they need to change. Publish actual project results when available; do not invent savings, testimonials or client endorsements.

First 90 days and success measures

  1. Weeks 1–2: establish current search and enquiry baselines. Refine the bilingual integration pages with a distinct automation offer and clearer proof. Search Console query data is needed to validate actual search impressions and clicks; none was available in this investigation.
  2. Weeks 3–6: publish one substantiated project write-up and the decision article above in both languages. Test the AI-app review section. Record the service, language, fit and outcome of enquiries.
  3. Weeks 7–12: compare qualified enquiries and agreed projects. Improve the pages attracting relevant queries. Create a separate automation page if it warrants distinct content. Keep low-volume pages when they produce good enquiries.

The primary measure is qualified enquiries leading to agreed scopes and paid projects. Count an enquiry as qualified when it describes a real software need within your skills, scope and availability. Supporting measures are service-page search clicks, relevant buyer queries and enquiry actions by page and language. Establish the baseline before numerical growth targets.

The existing custom Analytics Engine events record page and language, but do not connect visitor journeys or organic acquisition sources. Form success represents an accepted submission request rather than a unique qualified lead or confirmed email delivery. Do not label those totals as organic client conversions. Use manual lead qualification and Search Console separately; request further attribution only if needed.
