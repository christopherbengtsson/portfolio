# Competitor observations — 26 September 2026

Selected comparison set, not a ranked top five. Sources are provider self-descriptions; credentials, outcomes and customer claims have not been independently verified. Search exposure is a dated sample. Domain authority, backlink profiles, technical performance and competitor schema were not measured.

| Provider | Observed positioning and evidence | Lesson for Christopher |
|---|---|---|
| [HejNord](https://hejnord.se/tjanster/api-integrationer/) | Dedicated integration page explains fit, process, failure handling, ownership, price drivers and links to project examples. | Service pages should resolve buying questions and connect to proof. Do not copy its pricing or claim its platform experience. |
| [Altromatic](https://www.altromatic.com/) | Independent Stockholm consultant frames React/full-stack work around recognizable product problems and direct access to the engineer. | A named person can compete on clarity and ownership without presenting as a larger agency. |
| [Anton Wester](https://antonwester.se/) | Swedish consultant page identifies location/remote scope, technologies, named project examples and questions about engagement. | Pair capability with evidence and honest geographic scope. Christopher has since confirmed Gothenburg, Sweden, with remote delivery. |
| [Balr](https://balrconsulting.com/integrationskonsulter.html) | Integration consultant landing page appeared in the direct Google integration query and its visible AI Overview sources. | Hiring-oriented service content can be cited. Christopher needs a more focused offer than enterprise consultant matching. |
| [Kumpan](https://www.kumpan.se/fullstack-konsulter) | Full-stack consultant page appeared in the direct React/deltid query and its AI Overview; the result describes flexible engagements. | Explain how part-time collaboration works, while recognizing that this query also attracts job-seekers. |

An additional experimental-category result was [Abdur Rehman's prototype-to-production service](https://abdurrehman.dev/services/prototype-to-production). Its search excerpt specifically names Lovable, Bolt, Replit and Cursor; direct retrieval failed, so only that excerpt was used. Alongside other specialist results, it indicates existing competition rather than an untouched niche.

Christopher's current advantage is attributable experience across interfaces, backend services, data and integrations. The gap is the lack of dedicated pages that connect an individual buyer problem to that evidence. Publish genuine decisions and results, keeping employment history distinct from independent client engagements.

Given the confirmed full-time allocation elsewhere, borrow the clarity of these offers without copying their capacity commitments. Focused service routes should sit underneath a broad senior software engineer identity and invite smaller assignments.
